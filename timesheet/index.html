Calendar Timesheet is a Google Sheets add-on that turns your Google Calendar events into a clean timesheet and a per-client billing summary, ready to paste into an invoice. No timers, no new app.

Get started free See pricing

What it does

Match events to clients

Write simple rules like acme => Acme Corp | 120, or start an event title with [Client]. Each event lands under the right client with the right rate.

Bill the way you invoice

Round to 6, 15 or 30 minutes, up or to the nearest step. All-day events and meetings you declined are skipped automatically.

Two sheets, one click

A detailed Timesheet (date, time, client, hours, amount) and a Client Summary with total hours and amount per client for any date range.

Get started in 2 minutes

  1. Click Make a copy below. You get your own Google Sheet with Calendar Timesheet built in.
  2. In your copy, choose Extensions → Calendar Timesheet → Open. The first time, Google asks you to authorize the tool to read your calendar (read-only) and edit this sheet.
  3. Pick a calendar and a date range, add your client rules, and click Generate timesheet.

FAQ

Do you store my calendar?

No. Events are read inside your own Google account while the report is generated and written only to your spreadsheet. They are never sent to our servers. See the privacy policy.

How do I activate Pro?

After purchase you receive a license key by email. Paste it into the add-on sidebar and click Activate.

How do I cancel?

Open the customer portal (also linked in the add-on sidebar and your receipt email), sign in with your purchase email and click Cancel Subscription.
