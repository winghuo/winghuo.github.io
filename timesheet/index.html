Questions

Do you store my calendar?

No. Events are read inside your own Google account while the report is generated and written only to your spreadsheet. They are never sent to our servers. See the privacy policy.

What does "events per report" mean?

Each time you generate a timesheet, the free version includes up to 30 matching calendar events. Pro has no limit.

How do I activate Pro?

After purchase you receive a license key by email. Paste it into the add-on sidebar and click Activate.

How do I cancel?

Open the customer portal (also linked in the add-on sidebar and your receipt email), sign in with your purchase email and click Cancel Subscription.
